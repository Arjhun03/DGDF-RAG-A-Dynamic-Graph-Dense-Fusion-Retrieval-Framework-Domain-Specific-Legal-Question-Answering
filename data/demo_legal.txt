THE DEMO EDUCATIONAL ACT, 2026

Section 1. Purpose
This Act establishes an educational framework for demonstration of the DGDF-RAG system.

Section 2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Act, "Authority" means the educational authority established under this Act.
Clause 2(1). The Authority shall maintain records required under this Act.

Section 3. Duties of the Authority
The Authority shall publish an annual report and maintain the records described in Section 2.
Clause 3(1). The Authority may issue procedural guidelines consistent with this Act.

Section 4. Exception
Nothing in Section 3 requires publication of information that is confidential under an applicable rule.
Clause 4(1). The exception in this Section applies only to confidential information.

Section 5. Amendment
The Authority may recommend amendments to this Act in accordance with the prescribed procedure.
